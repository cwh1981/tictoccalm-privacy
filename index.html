Tic Toc Calm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

Summary

Tic Toc Calm does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app is a self-contained relaxation metronome. It works entirely on your device, requires no account, and connects to no servers of ours.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, identifiers, usage analytics, or any other personal information. Your taps and session activity stay on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking. We do not sell or share any data because we do not collect any.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app is safe for users of all ages with respect to privacy.

Changes
